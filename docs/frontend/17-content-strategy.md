# 17. Content Strategy

The copywriting on the VoyageAI marketing site follows a strict structure:
- **Headline**: 3 to 6 words. Punchy. Action-oriented.
- **Subheadline**: 1 to 2 sentences explaining the "How" and "Why".
- **Call to Action**: High-contrast, descriptive (e.g., "Start Planning" rather than "Click Here").

We NEVER use playful or overly quirky language. The tone is always premium, professional, and confident.
