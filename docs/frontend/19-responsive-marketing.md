# 19. Responsive Marketing

The marketing site must look flawless on all devices.

## Mobile Flow
- The top navbar collapses into a Hamburger menu.
- The 2-column grid in the Hero and Showcase sections stacks vertically (text on top, image/preview on bottom).
- The 4-step "How It Works" timeline loses its horizontal connecting line and stacks vertically.
- Generous padding (`py-32`) is reduced to (`py-24`) to preserve screen real estate.
